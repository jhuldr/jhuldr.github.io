Learning, Discovery, Reasoning Group @ JHU

The LDR Group at JHU conducts interdisciplinary research in AI, with a focus on Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Healthcare & Biomedical Applications. We develop AI systems that learn from complex, multimodal, and longitudinal data, discover meaningful biological and clinical patterns, and ultimately reason for informed decision-making.

Our long-term goal is to improve healthcare outcomes by:
-> Developing foundational methods for complex biomedical data;
-> Translating methodological advances into tools that support diagnosis, prognosis, treatment planning, and clinical decision-making.

Our work spans the full translational spectrum, from foundational machine learning and algorithm development to clinical validation and real-world deployment. We publish across leading venues in machine learning (e.g., NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML), computer vision (e.g., CVPR, ICCV, ECCV), and medical imaging (e.g., MICCAI, IPMI, IEEE TMI).
